🛍️ CornerShop

A comprehensive Django-based multi-vendor e-commerce marketplace that connects local businesses with customers, enabling seamless online shopping experiences.

Python Django 5.0.2 SQLite/MySQL HTML/CSS Bootstrap Icons

📋 Project Overview

CornerShop transforms traditional local shopping into a modern digital marketplace where business owners can register businesses, manage inventory, and track sales, while customers enjoy a unified shopping experience across different sellers.

The platform supports multiple vendors with role-based access, real-time inventory tracking, and a comprehensive dashboard for business analytics.

🎥 Demo Video

✨ Key Features

Multi-Vendor System

Complete business registration, approval workflow, and independent product management for each vendor.

E-Commerce Functionality

Full product catalog with category filtering, search, shopping cart, and secure checkout process.

Inventory Management

Real-time stock tracking, low stock alerts, automatic deduction on purchase, and prevent overselling.

User Management

User profiles with avatar upload, shipping addresses, order history, and password management.

Business Dashboard

Product CRUD operations, order tracking, sales analytics, and low stock alerts for business owners.

Secure Authentication

Role-based access control with different views for customers, business owners, and administrators.

🏗️ System Architecture

Django Apps Structure

  • conerapp: Core application with authentication, product catalog, and business management
  • cart: Session-based shopping cart with real-time updates
  • payment: Checkout workflow and order management

Database Models

  • UserProfile: Extended user information
  • Business: Business entities with approval
  • Product: Product catalog with stock
  • Order/OrderItem: Purchase tracking
